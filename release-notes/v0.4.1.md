# Debt Free v0.4.1

- Purchase entry no longer auto-focuses on open, avoiding the mobile zoom/jump behavior.
- Mobile modal controls use 16px form text and viewport-safe scrolling.
- Added Current activity and Historical entry modes to purchases and card payments.
- Historical purchases contribute to spending/category insights without changing current bank or card balances.
- Historical card payments contribute to payment-history insights without changing current bank or card balances.
- Added user-selectable dates for both purchases and card payments.
